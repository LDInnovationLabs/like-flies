Like Flies

Play as a fly or as the giant human. Flies stick to anything they land on, steal food from the snack cart, and carry it to the trash can. The human stands in one spot and swats, crushes, and claps flies.

How to fly

Push the air away from where you want to go. Throw your arms the opposite way. A harder throw gives a bigger dash.

Lift
Flap down hard. While gliding, only a strong stroke lifts you; gentle flaps power you forward instead.
Dash
Throw both arms one way to dash the other way. Throw right to dash left.
Drop
Throw both hands up over your head.
Glide
Hold your arms out. You buzz toward wherever you look, up and down included. No flapping needed.
Power
While gliding, flap your arms up and down or flutter your hands. Faster flapping goes faster. Stop and you coast, then settle back to glide speed.
Dive
Glide and look down for extra speed.
Rest
Stop flapping and you hover in place, drifting a little.
Brake
Drop your arms to your sides for a soft stop.
Turn
While gliding, dip one wing. Or flick the right thumbstick.
Settings
B or Y opens settings in VR. With bare hands, pinch your left thumb and finger for one second.
Reset
A or X, or the Reset button in settings.
Stick
You stick to anything you touch, including the human. Flap or dash to take off.
Steal
Land on food on the snack cart to grab a chunk, then carry it to the trash can. You're slower while carrying.

How to be the human

Stand
Stay on your spot. You can turn all the way around. If you step too far, the view dims. A or X re-centers your spot.
Swat
Hit a fly in the air to knock it away and stun it. It drops any food. That's not a kill.
Crush
Squash a fly that's sitting on something, including your own arm, the cart, or the food.
Clap
Clap a fly between your hands.
Swatter
Reach for a swatter on the cart and hold the grip button, or make a fist with bare hands. Let go to put it back.
Bites
A fly sitting on your hand bites, and you drop your swatter.
Win
The human wins when every fly is out of lives. The flies win when all the food is in the trash can.
Tuning
Best time: none yet Screen as fly: Space flap · W glide · Q/E dash · drag to look. As human: click swat · Space clap · G swatter
