Like Flies

You're a fly in a giant backyard. Flap your arms to fly through 10 rings, from under the picnic table to the giant's nose. This test is only about one thing: does flying feel good?

Checking for a VR headset…

How to fly

Flap
Swing both arms down hard. Faster swings lift you higher.
Glide
Hold both arms straight out at shoulder height.
Turn
While gliding, dip one wing. Or flick the right thumbstick.
Reset
Press A or X to start over.
Guide
Your left wrist shows the next ring and your time.
Tuning
Raise it if flying feels exhausting.
Lower feels floaty. Higher feels heavy and twitchy.
Higher stops you faster and lowers your top speed.
