How to fly

Push the air away from where you want to go. Flapping always gives power. The way your palms face decides where it pushes you.

Hover
Arms out, palms down, flap.
Forward
Tilt your palms back as you flap.
Dive
Arms out, look down. You trade height for speed.
Climb
Arms out, look up, keep flapping.
Glide
Arms out, stop flapping. Your glide bends toward where you look.
Back
Hands together in front, palms forward, flap.
Brake
Tuck your arms in and stop flapping.
Turn
While gliding, dip one wing. Or flick the right thumbstick.
Settings
B or Y opens settings in VR. With bare hands, pinch your left thumb and finger for one second.
Reset
A or X, or the Reset button in settings.
Tuning
Best time: none yet Screen: Space flap · W forward · A/D turn · drag to look
