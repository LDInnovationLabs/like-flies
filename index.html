Like Flies

A backyard playground for tiny flies. Race the ring course through a chicken coop, under a lawn chair, past a rubber duck and over the sunflowers. The giant human walks the yard with two swatters, and practice flies stream around the course.

How to fly

Push the air away from where you want to go. Throw your arms the opposite way. A harder throw gives a bigger dash.

Lift
Flap down hard. While gliding, only a strong stroke lifts you; gentle flaps power you forward instead.
Dash
Throw both arms one way to dash the other way. Throw right to dash left.
Drop
Throw both hands up over your head.
Glide
Hold your arms out. You buzz toward wherever you look, up and down included. No flapping needed.
Power
While gliding, flap your arms up and down or flutter your hands. Faster flapping goes faster. Stop and you coast, then settle back to glide speed.
Dive
Glide and look down for extra speed.
Rest
Stop flapping and you hover in place, drifting a little.
Brake
Drop your arms to your sides for a soft stop.
Turn
While gliding, dip one wing. Or flick the right thumbstick.
Rings
Ten rings make a loop through the yard: over the grill, through the doghouse, under a lawn chair, through the chicken coop, over the fire, past the tricycle, over the duck, down the corn row, over the tallest sunflower and under a table.
Settings
B or Y opens settings in VR. With bare hands, pinch your left thumb and finger for one second.
Reset
A or X, or the Reset button in settings.
Land
Touch anything to land on it, including the human. Your six feet plant on the surface.
Crawl
Left thumbstick walks you along the surface: over edges, up walls and underneath. With bare hands, reach a hand out in front of you toward where you want to walk; a yellow arrow on the surface shows the way. Pull your hands in to stop. The whole world turns with the surface, so under the flamingo the yard is upside down.
Take off
Flap down hard toward the surface, or pull a trigger. The world levels back to normal behind a short dark vignette.
Eat
Land on food (or crawl onto it) to grab a piece, then survive until the timer runs out to eat it and score a point. You cruise slower while carrying, but flapping still pushes at full strength.
Swatted
Three air swats and you're out (one life). Getting squashed while you're sitting on something, or clapped, is instant. A swat makes you drop your food.

How to be the human

Stand
Wherever you're standing when you become the human is your spot at the grill. If you step too far in your room, the view dims. A or X re-centers your spot where you stand.
Walk
Left thumbstick walks you around the yard (forward, back and sideways). Right thumbstick still snap-turns. Big things like chairs, the coop and the house block you. Plants don't.
Cover
Swatters stop at chairs, the grill, the coop and other solid things, so a fly hiding under a chair is safe until you reach around.
Swat
Hit a fly in the air to knock it back, stun it, and make it drop food. Three air swats eliminate it.
Crush
Squash a fly that has landed on something, including your own arm or the food. One hit.
Clap
Clap a fly between your hands.
Swatters
Two hang on hooks beside the grill. Grab one in each hand with the grip button, or make a fist with bare hands. Let go to put it back.
Style
Knock a fly into the grill or fire pit (Overcooked), the wind chime, the red cup, or the spider web for a style bonus.
Bites
A fly sitting on your hand bites, and you drop your swatter.
Win
Test rules for now: the human wins when every fly player is out of lives. The flies win when all the food is eaten.
